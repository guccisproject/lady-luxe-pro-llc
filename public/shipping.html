Shipping Policy

How, when, and where your order travels — from our hands in Titusville to your door.

Last updated September 23, 2026

Where we ship

Lady Katt Luxe ships to addresses throughout the United States, including all 50 states, Washington, D.C., and APO/FPO/DPO military addresses. We do not ship internationally at this time.

Processing time

Every order is packed by hand. Orders are processed within 1–3 business days (Monday through Friday, excluding U.S. federal holidays). Orders placed on weekends or holidays begin processing the next business day. During peak seasons and promotions, processing may take a little longer — we’ll let you know if there’s a delay.

Order tracking

As soon as your order ships, we’ll email you a confirmation with your tracking number. If you haven’t received it within 3 business days of placing your order, please check your spam folder or contact us.

Address accuracy

Please double-check your shipping address at checkout. We are not responsible for orders delivered to an incorrect address provided by the customer. If you notice an error, contact us right away — we’ll do our best to update it before your order ships. Packages returned to us as undeliverable can be reshipped at the customer’s expense.

Lost, stolen, or delayed packages

Once a package is marked as delivered by the carrier, it is considered delivered. If your tracking shows delivered but you can’t find your package, please check with neighbours and your building’s mail area, then contact us within 7 days and we’ll help you open a claim with the carrier.

Damaged in transit

We package every piece carefully, but if your order arrives damaged, please email us within 7 days of delivery with your order number and photos of the item and packaging. We’ll arrange a replacement or refund at no cost to you.

Sales tax

Applicable sales tax is calculated at checkout based on your shipping address, where required by law.
