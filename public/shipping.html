Shipping Policy

How and when your order travels — from Titusville to your door.

Last updated August 1, 2026

At Lady Katt Luxe LLC, we are committed to delivering your luxury essentials safely, securely, and in a timely manner.

Processing time

Orders are typically processed within 1–3 business days (Monday–Friday, excluding holidays) after payment confirmation. During peak seasons or promotional periods, processing may take slightly longer.

Shipping rates & delivery estimates

Shipping charges are calculated at checkout based on your location and the shipping method selected. Estimated delivery times vary by carrier and destination and typically range from 3–10 business days after processing, for domestic shipments within the United States.

Order tracking

Once your order ships, you will receive a confirmation email with tracking information so you can monitor your delivery status.

Shipping delays

Lady Katt Luxe LLC is not responsible for delays caused by the shipping carrier, weather, customs, or other events outside our control. If your order is significantly delayed, please contact us and we will do our best to assist.

Lost or damaged packages

If your package arrives damaged or is lost in transit, please contact us within 7 days of the expected delivery date so we can file a claim with the carrier and work toward a resolution.

Incorrect shipping information

Please double-check your shipping address at checkout. Lady Katt Luxe LLC is not responsible for orders delayed or misdelivered due to incorrect or incomplete address information provided by the customer.
