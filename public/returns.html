Returns & Refunds

Changed your mind? Here’s how returns, exchanges, and refunds work.

Last updated September 23, 2026

Our promise

We want you to love every piece you receive. If something isn’t quite right, you may return eligible items within 30 days of delivery for a refund to your original payment method or an exchange.

Eligibility

To be eligible for a return, items must be:

  • Unused, unworn, and unwashed, in the same condition you received them;
  • In their original packaging, with all tags, pouches, boxes, and inserts included;
  • Returned within 30 days of the delivery date shown on your tracking.

Gift sets must be returned complete. Individual items from a gift set cannot be returned separately.

Final-sale items

For health and hygiene reasons, the following cannot be returned unless they arrive damaged or defective:

  • Pierced earrings;
  • Beauty, skincare, cosmetics, and fragrance that have been opened, used, or had their seal broken;
  • Items marked “Final Sale” at the time of purchase;
  • Gift cards.

How to start a return

  1. Email contact@ladykattluxe.shop with your order number, the item(s) you’d like to return, and the reason.
  2. We’ll reply within one business day with return instructions and the return address. Please don’t send items back without contacting us first — unannounced returns can’t always be processed.
  3. Pack items securely and ship them using a trackable service. We recommend keeping your receipt until your refund is issued.

Return shipping

Return shipping costs are the customer’s responsibility, except where an item arrived damaged, defective, or incorrect — in those cases we’ll send you a prepaid label. Original shipping charges are non-refundable, and if your order qualified for complimentary shipping, the standard shipping cost may be deducted if the return brings the order below the $75 threshold.

Refunds

Once your return arrives, we’ll inspect it and email you to confirm whether it has been approved. Approved refunds are issued to your original payment method within 5–10 business days. Depending on your bank or card issuer, it may take a few additional days for the credit to appear.

Items returned used, damaged, or incomplete may be refused or receive a partial refund.

Exchanges

Would you prefer a different size, shade, or piece? Let us know when you contact us. Once your return is received and approved, we’ll ship the replacement (subject to availability). If the new item costs more, we’ll send you a secure link for the difference; if it costs less, we’ll refund the balance.

Damaged, defective, or incorrect items

If your order arrives damaged, defective, or isn’t what you ordered, please contact us within 7 days of delivery with your order number and photos. We’ll make it right with a replacement or full refund, including shipping.

Late or missing refunds

If you haven’t received your refund after 10 business days, please first check with your bank or card issuer, then contact us and we’ll look into it right away.
