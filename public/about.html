Our story

Made for the everyday

Lady Katt Luxe was founded on a simple belief: luxury should be something you live with, not something you wait for.

We curate a refined collection of home décor, fashion, beauty, jewelry, and lifestyle essentials — each piece hand-selected for its quality, its craftsmanship, and the quiet confidence it brings to your day. Nothing earns a place in our collection unless it meets our standard: beautiful to look at, a pleasure to use, and made to last.

From our home in Titusville, Florida, we serve customers across the country with the same care we put into every selection — elegant packaging, attentive service, and fast, reliable shipping to your door.

What we stand for

Our standard

Curated, never crowded

We would rather offer a few exceptional pieces than hundreds of forgettable ones. Every item is chosen with intention, so browsing feels effortless and every choice feels right.

Quality you can feel

We look closely at materials, finishes, and construction — the weight of a glass, the clasp on a bracelet, the throw of a candle — so what arrives at your door feels as good as it looks.

Considered pricing

We source carefully and keep our overhead lean, so the pieces you love are priced for real life. Treating yourself shouldn’t require a special occasion.

Service with grace

From the first question to the moment your package arrives, you’ll be looked after by people who care. If something isn’t right, we’ll make it right.

From Titusville to your door

Proudly based on Florida’s Space Coast

Lady Katt Luxe LLC is a Florida limited liability company headquartered in Titusville. Every order is packed by hand and shipped to customers throughout the United States.
