Privacy & Cookie Policy

What we collect, why we collect it, and how we keep it safe.

Last updated September 23, 2026

Overview

Lady Katt Luxe LLC respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit ladykattluxe.shop or shop with us, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Order information — your name, email, phone number, shipping and billing address, and the items you purchase. Payment card details are entered directly with Stripe and are never received or stored by us.
  • Messages — the details you share when you contact us through our form, email, or phone.
  • Technical information — basic data your browser sends automatically, such as IP address, browser type, and pages visited, which our hosting provider may log for security and reliability.

How we use your information

  • To process, ship, and support your orders, including returns and exchanges;
  • To respond to your questions and requests;
  • To prevent fraud and keep our Site secure;
  • To meet our legal, tax, and accounting obligations.

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We will only send you marketing emails if you have opted in, and you can unsubscribe at any time.

Who we share it with

  • Stripe, Inc. — to process payments securely (Stripe Privacy Policy);
  • Shipping carriers — to deliver your order;
  • Service providers — such as our website host and email provider, who process data only on our behalf;
  • Authorities — where required by law or to protect our rights.

Cookies & local storage

Cookies and similar technologies (such as your browser’s local storage) are small files that help a website remember information. We keep their use to a minimum.

We do not currently use advertising or analytics cookies. If we add optional cookies in the future, they will only be set if you choose “Accept all” in our cookie banner, and this policy will be updated. You can change your choice at any time via Cookie Settings, and you can block or delete cookies in your browser settings (though your bag may not work without essential storage).

Data retention

We keep order records for as long as needed to fulfil your order and to meet legal, tax, and accounting requirements (generally up to seven years). Messages are kept only as long as needed to help you.

Security

Our Site is served over an encrypted connection, and payments are handled by Stripe, a PCI-DSS Level 1 certified provider. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

Your choices & rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information we hold about you, subject to legal exceptions. Depending on your state of residence, you may have additional privacy rights. To make a request, email contact@ladykattluxe.shop. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

Children

Our Site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “last updated” date above shows when it was last revised.
