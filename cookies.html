Cookie Policy

How we use cookies and similar technologies on our website.

Last updated September 23, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Lady Katt Luxe LLC (“we,” “us,” “our”) uses cookies and similar technologies on our website.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites function properly and remember information about your visit.

How we use cookies

At this time, Lady Katt Luxe LLC uses cookies only for essential website functionality, including:

  • Remembering your cookie preference
  • Enabling secure checkout
  • Maintaining basic site security and performance

We do not currently use cookies for advertising, third-party tracking, or analytics purposes. If this changes in the future, we will update this policy accordingly and, where required by law, request your consent before using non-essential cookies.

Managing cookies

Because our site currently relies on essential cookies only, disabling them through your browser settings may affect core functionality such as the checkout process. You can manage or delete cookies at any time through your browser settings. You can also review your choice in our cookie banner at any time via Cookie Settings.

Third-party cookies

Our payment processor(s) and website platform may place their own essential cookies necessary to process transactions securely. These are governed by the respective third party’s own privacy and cookie policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices. The “last updated” date at the top will reflect the most recent revision.
